MasteryQuiz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 Oct 26

MasteryQuiz ("the app") is a quiz-making and exam-practice app for Android. It is provided by James McCarthy, based in the United Kingdom ("we", "us"). You can contact us at MasteryQuiz26@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-up.
  • Your quizzes, bookmarks, scores, history and settings are stored on your device only. We do not receive them.
  • The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show them (see section 5).
  • If you tap "Report this question", we receive the reported question and your reason (see section 6).
  • If you use AI features, your text may be processed as described in section 4, depending on the AI option you choose.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following in the app's private storage on your phone: quiz sets you create or import, bookmarks, quiz progress, scores and attempt history, your settings, and, if you enter one, your Gemini API key. This information stays on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the app deletes it. If you use "Export backup", the file you create is saved wherever you choose, and you are responsible for it.

When you import a PDF, its text is extracted on your device.

3. Information we collect

We do not run analytics, we do not use accounts, and we do not collect your name, email address or location. The only information that reaches us directly is the content of a report you choose to send (section 6).

4. AI features and how your text is handled

The app can generate quiz questions in three ways. You choose which one to use in Settings.

  • On-device AI (Gemini Nano): on supported phones, text is processed on your device by Google's Android AI services. Your text is not sent to us.
  • Gemini cloud API with your own key: if you enter your own Google Gemini API key, the topics, notes or text you submit are sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API under your own Google account and Google's terms and privacy policy. Your text does not pass through any server of ours. Content you send on the free tier may be used by Google as described in Google's terms, so do not submit confidential material.
  • Copy and paste: the app can prepare a prompt that you copy into another chatbot of your choice (for example Microsoft Copilot) and paste the reply back. That service's own terms and privacy policy then apply.

AI-generated questions may be inaccurate or inappropriate. Please check them before relying on them, and use the report button if you see problematic content.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob: a banner on the home screen and an occasional full-screen ad after you finish a quiz. To provide and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the UK and European Economic Area), the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices where this option is shown. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). We do not sell your personal information.

6. Reporting AI-generated content

If you tap "Report this question", the app sends us: the question text, its answer options and explanation, the reason you selected, any note you type, the quiz title, the app version, the platform (Android) and the time. We use reports to improve content filtering and moderation. Reports are delivered to us through Formspree, which acts as our processor. Please do not include personal information in the note. We keep reports for 12 months and then delete them. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and compliant with app store rules.

7. Permissions

The app uses internet access (to reach AI services, show ads and send reports) and the Android advertising ID (used by AdMob, section 5). It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of personal information we hold about you, and to data portability. Because we hold almost no personal information, the practical request is usually to delete a report you sent: email us and describe it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Security

Data sent from the app to AI services, ad services and our report endpoint travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and for significant changes we will tell you in the app or the store listing.
